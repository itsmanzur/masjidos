FAQ

Common questions

Does prayer calculation need an API?

No. MasjidOS calculates locally from your coordinates and method. Optional SalahAPI enrichment can be used when configured — core prayer works offline-first.

Does it work with Elementor?

Yes. Paste shortcodes into an Elementor Shortcode or HTML widget. Gutenberg blocks are also available in Free.

Is Free forever free?

Yes. The public mosque toolkit stays on WordPress.org. Pro is optional for donations, accounts, members, and premium designs.

Does Pro need Free?

Yes. Activate MasjidOS Free first, then MasjidOS Pro. Pro extends Free; it does not replace it.

What is Pro Network?

A multi-mosque / WordPress multisite-oriented license for running Pro across N sites. Contact sales for N and terms.
