FAQ

Straight answers

Does prayer calculation need an API?

No. Local solar calculation is the default. Optional Aladhan or a CSV timetable you upload. Core prayer works without a network call.

Is Free forever free?

Yes. The public mosque toolkit stays on WordPress.org. Pro is a separate plugin for committee money, CRM, school, and hall booking.

Does Pro need Free?

Yes. Activate MasjidOS first, then MasjidOS Pro. Pro sets MASJIDOS_PRO_ACTIVE and extends Free through filters.

bKash and Nagad?

In Pro, with sandbox or live keys. Cash and bank pledges stay pending until a treasurer confirms.

What is Pro Network?

A multi-mosque / multisite-oriented license. Contact sales for site count and terms. Price is a placeholder here.
